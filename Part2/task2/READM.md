***Smart Greenhouse Monitoring system using AI and IoT:***
***Introduction***
* By protecting crops from harsh conditions and pests, greenhouses assist farmers in growing their crops. However, maintaining a greenhouse requires continuously monitoring the soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and sunlight, which can be burdening and prone to human error.

* This project propose SmartGrow, a Smart Greenhouse System that makes use of automation and sensors to make it simple for farmers to keep an eye on and manage their greenhouse environment. This system will lower the chance of loss, save energy and water, and enhance crop health.

***Objective***
✅ Monitor and control warehouse conditions (temperature, humidity, air quality) to preserve perishable crops.
✅ Automate alerts and responses when conditions deviate from set thresholds.
✅ Use data analytics to predict harsh conditions nut suit for crops growth.

***Sensors Needed:***

    a. Temperature sensor : Track the greenhouse's internal temperature
    b. Humidity sensor : monitor the amount of moisture in the air.
    c. CO₂ sensor : Monitor the levels of air
    d. Soil moisture sensor : determine whether the soil is too wet or too dry.
    e. Light intensity sensor : Determine the amount of sunlight

***How SmartGrow Operates: Sensors Placed in the Greenhouse***

* As the censors collect data, A central system (IoT platform) receives the real-time data from the sensors.

* Farmers can use a computer, tablet, or phone to view the current status.

***Automated Management***

* The system can activate fans or open vents if the temperature rises too high.

* It can initiate irrigation on its own if the soil becomes excessively dry.

* When human intervention is required, the system can send out alerts.

***Information to Improve Farming***

These information aids farmers in making more informed choices regarding planting, watering, and harvesting.

***Advantages of SmartGrow***
    * SmartGrow's advantages include healthier crops and consistently ideal growing conditions.

    * Conserve Water—Just use water when necessary

    * Saves Time — Less manual adjustment and verification

    * Increased Production – More and superior quality produce

    * Easy to Use: Basic dashboard and notifications

***Conclusion***
* SmartGrow is like having a smart assistant for your greenhouse — always watching, always helping. It gives farmers peace of mind, better harvests, and more control over their farming.
