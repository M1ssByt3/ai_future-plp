**Q1: Explain how Edge AI reduces latency and enhances privacy compared to cloud-based AI. Provide a real-world example (e.g., autonomous drones).**

* Latency is reduced on edge AI devices since data is locally processed since it does not travel through netork to a remote server and back. The delay between input and response is small. In autonomous drones example,  it requires rapid responses for the drone to make quick decision while doing surveillance like avoiding obstacles, terrain changes. edge ai enables faster data processing and responsing tomake such decisions.
* Same case as enhancing data privacy, data on transit is at risk of interceptions over the network. On edge AI, local data procession means only minimal metadata leaves the device. Users have more visibility and control when data stays on their personal device. Edge AI can help meet data protection regulations like GDPR: Minimizes data sharing and ensures data sovereignty. Cloud AI on the contrary, data is at risk of attacks while its on the internet on transit and their storage.

**Q2: Compare Quantum AI and classical AI in solving optimization problems. What industries could benefit most from Quantum AI?**

    The way that classical and quantum AI process data determines how they approach optimization.  Using bits and techniques like heuristics, gradient descent, or brute-force search, classical AI solves optimization issues. This approach is effective for many real-world jobs but slows down and becomes ineffective as the problem size increases, particularly for complex, multidimensional scenarios.  Quantum AI, on the other hand, applies methods like quantum annealing and the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) to explore large solution spaces significantly more quickly since it uses qubits that can exist in several states concurrently.

**Q3: Discuss the societal impact of Human-AI collaboration in healthcare. How might it transform roles like radiologists or nurses?**
AI acts as a decision-support tool, not a replacement for radiologists, freeing them up to concentrate on complex cases and patient interaction. AI-powered monitoring tools for nurses can predict complications and track patient vitals in real time, allowing for early intervention and minimizing workload. While AI takes care of repetitive or data-intensive tasks, this partnership frees up healthcare professionals to focus more on providing compassionate, human-centered care. Socially, this could help address labor shortages and enhance access to healthcare, particularly in underprivileged areas.
